Bitcoin Tops $79,000 in Its Best Week Since 2023

NEW YORK — Bitcoin climbed as high as $79,400 on Friday and is up nearly 24% since Monday, its best week since 2023. The spark was the U.S. Treasury’s move to expand long-dated bond buybacks — not money-printing, but a debt-management shift that eases long yields and softens the dollar. Leverage did the rest: about $3.3 billion in positions were liquidated Wednesday and another $1.24 billion over the next day, shorts making up $1.06 billion of it. Traders keep fading the move and keep being wrong. Two demand signals flicker green — spot ETFs took in $606 million on Aug. 20, and CryptoQuant’s spot-demand gauge is near turning positive for the first time since February — but the gains were built on forced buying, not fresh holders.

Why it matters: the number jumped because Washington adjusted how it buys its own bonds, not because new people chose to hold bitcoin.
