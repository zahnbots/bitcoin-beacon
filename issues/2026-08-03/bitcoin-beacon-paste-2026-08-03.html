A Chinese Insurer Swapped Shares for a Bitcoin Treasury

SHENZHEN — A Chinese insurance-technology firm has built a bitcoin treasury without spending a dollar to do it. Zhibao Technology, listed on the Nasdaq, agreed on July 31 to sell $154.7 million of new stock — and take payment in bitcoin, 2,380 coins, valued at a fixed $65,000 each.

No cash was raised and none was spent buying on the open market; the bitcoin simply moved onto the balance sheet as the price of the shares. The catch is control: the deal shrinks the board to five seats, pushes out four directors, the CEO and the CFO, and hands the incoming investors the run of the company. The coins, Zhibao says, will fund AI work in its insurance arm.

Why it matters: a company can now acquire bitcoin faster than it can raise the money to buy it — but the shortcut can cost it the boardroom.
