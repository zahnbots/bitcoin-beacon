Bhutan Is Selling Its Bitcoin to Build a City

THIMPHU — On August 7 the Royal Government of Bhutan moved another 434 bitcoin, about $28 million, out of its treasury. The kingdom that quietly mined a billion-dollar stack from its Himalayan rivers has now sold roughly 70% of it — from more than 13,000 coins in October 2024 to an estimated 3,000–4,000 today. The sales run over the counter through the sovereign fund Druk Holding, in careful batches that barely move the price, and the money is widely understood to be building Gelephu Mindfulness City, an economic zone on the southern border. One catch: the mining that filled the vault has reportedly stopped, so this is a stock being drawn down with nothing refilling it.

Why it matters: a bitcoin reserve is a choice a government has to keep making, and Bhutan has decided a city today beats coins tomorrow.
