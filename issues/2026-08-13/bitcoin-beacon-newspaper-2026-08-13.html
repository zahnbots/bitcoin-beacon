Bhutan Is Selling Its Bitcoin to Build a City

The kingdom that mined a billion-dollar stack has sold roughly 70% of it — and the proceeds are pouring into a new city on its southern border.

THIMPHU — On August 7 the Royal Government of Bhutan moved another 434 bitcoin, about $28 million, out of its treasury. The kingdom that quietly mined a billion-dollar stack from its Himalayan rivers has now sold roughly 70% of it — from more than 13,000 coins in October 2024 to an estimated 3,000–4,000 today.

The sales run over the counter through the sovereign fund Druk Holding, in careful batches that barely move the price, and the money is widely understood to be building Gelephu Mindfulness City, an economic zone on the southern border meant to pull Bhutan’s economy beyond hydropower and tourism. One catch: the mining that filled the vault has reportedly stopped, so this is a stock being drawn down with nothing refilling it. El Salvador buys and holds; Bhutan mined and is spending.

Why it matters: a bitcoin reserve is a choice a government has to keep making, and Bhutan has decided a city today beats coins tomorrow.

Network & Mining

GLOBAL. A critical bug in BTCPay Server — the open-source processor behind hundreds of thousands of merchants — let attackers steal the credential files that control a Lightning node, close its channels and sweep the funds. Versions before 2.4.2 were exploited before the patch shipped; developers cut off remote node access to stop the bleeding. Running your own payment rail means running a hot wallet on the internet. Markets & Institutions

TYSONS CORNER, Va. The company that made “never sell” a slogan now runs a $4.65 billion cash pile. Strategy lifted its dollar reserve by $650 million and spent $109 million buying back its own preferred stock — funded partly by selling bitcoin. The largest corporate holder is fortifying its balance sheet rather than stacking, a tell about how these treasury vehicles survive a flat market. Full story →

Policy & Nation-States

BRASÍLIA. Brazil’s central bank will make big crypto withdrawals wait. From January 2027, moving more than $10,000 in a day off a licensed platform — to a foreign exchange or your own wallet — triggers a mandatory 24-hour hold for a risk check. Officials call it fraud protection; for anyone using bitcoin to get money out of the system, it’s friction aimed at the exit. Full story →

The Take · Opinion

Holding Your Own Bitcoin Is Getting Harder

Three unrelated stories this week pointed the same way: a Brazilian rule that delays your withdrawal, an exploit that drained self-hosted Lightning nodes, a smoother path to swap coins into an ETF held by Coinbase. A regulator managing fraud, a project shipping a fix, an asset manager chasing flows — no conspiracy. But every path is being smoothed toward letting someone else hold your bitcoin, and roughened for holding it yourself.

Self-custody doesn’t lose to a better argument; it loses to a smoother alternative. The steelman is strong — most people are safer with a custodian than a hot wallet they half understand. It’s fine right up until the account is frozen or the withdrawal is delayed. Keep the exit open while it’s still free.
