Bitcoin Surges Past $72,000 as the Treasury Doubles Bond Buybacks

WASHINGTON — Bitcoin climbed more than 12% in a day, briefly clearing $72,000 for the first time since June, after the U.S. Treasury said it would double its long-dated bond buybacks to at least $4 billion per operation from September 9. The plan pulled the 30-year yield off a 2007 high near 5.34%, softened the dollar, and pushed money back into risk.

On leveraged venues the rally detonated roughly $1.4 billion of short positions in about an hour — a forced-buying cascade that turned a policy headline into a violent squeeze. Ether and crypto-linked stocks rose with it.

Why it matters: bitcoin's biggest moves now answer to Washington's debt plumbing, not to any new buyer of the coin.
