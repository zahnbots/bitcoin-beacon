The Take · Opinion

Wall Street Wants Bitcoin to Pay Interest. Ask Where It Comes From.

Regulators just cleared bitcoin ETFs that pay a yield, and the pitch writes itself: own bitcoin, earn on it too. One question settles most of the argument — where does the yield come from? Bitcoin pays no interest; to make it pay, someone lends your coins or writes options against them, putting a counterparty back between you and the asset. The 2022 graveyard — Celsius, BlockFi, Voyager — was built on exactly that promise, and this week's Coldcard hack is the other bookend: not-your-keys and not-your-node both bite. The honest counterpoint: a regulated, over-collateralized, transparent yield can pay a real premium, and institutions want income — but read the mechanics before the headline rate, because the moment bitcoin pays you interest, it is no longer only yours.
